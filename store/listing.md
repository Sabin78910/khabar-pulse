## Full description
Khabar Pulse (खबर पल्स) brings Nepal and world news together in one clean, fast feed.

Why it's different
• One story, many sources: when five outlets report the same event, you see it once, with "5 sources" and every outlet listed.
• Fast and light: opens instantly and works offline with your last news.
• Nepali and English: switch the app language, and choose stories in Nepali, English or both.
• Today in 60 seconds: the 8 biggest stories, one swipe each.
• Tabs for Top, Nepal, World, Business, Sports, Tech and Entertainment.
• Search, bookmarks, a gentle daily reading streak, dark and light themes.
• Bikram Sambat date and times in Nepali numerals.

Fair to publishers
We show only the headline, a short snippet, the image and the source name. Tap any story to read it on the
publisher's own site. Sources include Onlinekhabar, Setopati, Ratopati, The Kathmandu Post, The Himalayan Times,
BBC Nepali, Khabarhub, Nagarik, Annapurna Post, BBC, Al Jazeera, The Guardian and more.

No account. No tracking. Privacy policy: https://sabin78910.github.io/privacy-policy/
